# Privacy Policy — RPS Tactics

**Last updated:** 18 August 2026

This game is made by one person. This document describes exactly what the
game does — it was written from its source code, not copied from a template.

---

## In short

The game never asks for your name, email or phone number, and does not use
social network sign-in. Nothing that identifies you as a person is collected.

Three things leave your device: an anonymous device identifier, the display
name you choose, and your number of wins. In addition, the ad network
collects device data — and only if you allow it.

---

## Stored on your device

- points, level, matches played, wins, losses and draws;
- the language you selected;
- board view angle and zoom;
- whether music and sound are on;
- how many matches you have played (ads are shown based on this count);
- your answer about personalised ads.

This is kept in the app's storage on your device. Delete the game and it goes
with it.

If you link an account, the first item on this list — your game progress — is
also stored in the cloud, see below. Everything else never leaves your device.

---

## Sent to servers

### Anonymous sign-in

To play online and appear in the leaderboard, the game creates an account
without a password, tied to your device. No email, name or phone number is
requested or transmitted.

Technically it is a random string that cannot be traced back to a person.

If you reinstall the game you get a new account, and the previous progress and
leaderboard place become unreachable. Linking an account avoids this: both come
back on any device.

### Display name

By default the game calls you **Player** and appends a number so that
identical names do not clash. You can change it on the leaderboard screen.

**Your display name is visible to other players.** Do not put your real name,
email, phone number or anything else you would not share with strangers.

### Leaderboard

Only the number of wins over live players is sent. Wins over the computer
are not counted.

### Progress in the cloud

If you link an account, your game progress — points, level, matches played,
wins, losses and draws — is stored on Unity servers as well as on your device.
This is what makes progress survive a change of phone or a reinstall.

Without a linked account, progress stays on your device only.

### Account linking

Linking is optional. The game works without it.

If you do link, sign-in goes through a Unity account, and Unity passes only an
identifier to the game — that is what lets another device recognise you. Your
email and password are never passed to the game: Unity handles them on its own
side.

You can unlink the account in the game settings, in the same place you linked it.

### Online play

When you play against a live opponent, the following passes through servers:
the room name and password, the moves made during the match, and the data
needed to connect two devices. Moves are piece numbers and board squares,
nothing more.

All of the above is processed by **Unity Technologies**. Their policy:
https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

---

## Advertising

The game is free and shows ads between matches — after every second match,
starting with the third.

Ads are served by **Unity Ads**. The ad network collects device information:
model, operating system version, advertising identifier, and approximate
location derived from your network address. This is used to select ads and
count impressions.

**On first launch the game asks whether ads may be tailored using your data.**
Until you answer, the ad network is not started and collects nothing.

If you decline, ads remain but become random rather than tailored to you.
You can change your answer at any time: **Settings → Personalised ads**.

Unity Ads policy:
https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

## Children

The game is not directed primarily to children under 13 and is not marketed
to them.

## Your rights

**Withdraw consent for personalised ads** — in the game settings, at any time.

**Delete all data** — uninstall the game. Everything stored locally is removed
with it.

To have your display name and score removed from the leaderboard, write to the
address below. Please include your display name with its number — for example
`Player#1234` — as shown on the leaderboard screen.
